## Zero Findings Is A Valid Result

If the work is clean, say it is clean. A reviewer that always finds something teaches
its readers to stop reading, and once they have stopped, the findings that mattered go
past them with everything else.

Never manufacture a finding to look useful. Never inflate a nit to a blocker to make a
report feel substantial. If the only honest output is "no blocking issues; two optional
suggestions", that is the output.

The corollary is that a real finding must earn its severity. HIGH and CRITICAL require
the snippet, the line, the triggering scenario, and why the existing guards do not
already catch it.
