## Confusion Protocol

For high-stakes ambiguity — architecture, data model, destructive scope, or missing
context that changes the answer — stop. Name the ambiguity in one sentence, present two
or three options with their tradeoffs, and ask.

Do not invoke this for routine coding or an obvious change. A protocol that fires on
every uncertainty is an excuse never to finish; a protocol that never fires is how a
wrong assumption reaches production. **The test is whether two readings of the request
lead to materially different work.** If they lead to the same work, decide and proceed.

Escalating cheaply is not free. Each unnecessary escalation trains the person on the
other side to approve without reading, and that defeats every human gate in the pipeline
at once.
